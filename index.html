Terms of service

please read before ordering — this is binding

I will do

  • Any in-game Fortnite skin
  • Custom poses & multi-character scenes
  • Props, pickaxes & backblings
  • Alt / edit styles of a skin
  • Mild blood & horror lighting
  • PFPs, banners, thumbnails, montage art

I won't do

  • NSFW / suggestive posing
  • Hateful or discriminatory themes
  • Heavy gore
  • Modeling a skin from scratch
  • Custom skin designs / concepts
  • Anything meant to harass someone

Skins I can work with

  1. I only work with skins that are already in-game. I can't model or rip anything that hasn't been released.
  2. Some skins have parts that are hard to rig or pose. If yours is one of them I'll tell you before you pay.
  3. Everything I need from you before we start is listed in the info tab.

Payment

  1. All payments go through Buy Me a Coffee — I'll send you the link once we've agreed on the details.
  2. Payment is made upfront, before I start working.
  3. Your slot is only reserved once the payment clears.
  4. If it isn't paid within 7 days, the slot goes to the next person in queue.

Revisions

  1. 2 free revision rounds are included — one at the posing stage, one at the lighting stage.
  2. Small fixes (background colour, crop, camera nudges) are always free.
  3. Changing the skin or the whole scene after the pose is approved counts as a new commission.
  4. Extra revision rounds beyond the free ones are 2 USD each.

Refunds & cancellation

  1. Full refund if I haven't started yet.
  2. Partial refund based on progress once work has begun.
  3. No refunds after the final files are delivered.
  4. If a skin turns out to be impossible to pose properly, I'll cancel and refund you in full.
  5. I may cancel and refund if the request breaks these rules.

Usage & credit

  1. Renders are for personal use — PFPs, banners, thumbnails, posting them on your socials.
  2. No resale, no NFTs, and no selling the render as merch.
  3. Please credit me when you post it.
  4. Don't claim the render as your own work, re-upload it as yours, or edit it beyond simple cropping.
  5. Don't feed the files into AI training or image generators.
  6. I keep the right to post the render in my portfolio and on social media.

All Fortnite characters, skins and assets belong to Epic Games. I'm not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games — you're paying for my posing, scene building and render work, not for the character models themselves. By sending a commission request you confirm that you've read and agree to everything above.

How to order

five steps from idea to final render

  1. Reach out DM me on X or Discord with the skin name, the pose you want and any references.
  2. Quote & slot I reply within 48h with a final price and an estimated delivery date. If it works, I send you my Buy Me a Coffee link.
  3. Posing I set the skin up and pose it, then send you a quick viewport preview to approve. First revision round happens here.
  4. Scene & lighting Background, props and lighting go in, then a watermarked test render. Second revision round happens here.
  5. Delivery Final render at full resolution — no watermark, transparent version included, cropped for wherever you're posting it.
